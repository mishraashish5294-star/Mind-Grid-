How MindGrid works

Categories and levels

  • Mathematical reasoning — arithmetic fluency, algebra, matrices, logarithms, modular arithmetic, counting, probability and calculus.
  • Spatial reasoning — areas, coordinate transformations, 3D volumes, rotations, vectors and linear transformations.
  • Sequence reasoning — arithmetic and geometric progressions, recurrences, polynomial sequences, series and limits.

Foundation → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → Master. Each tier raises both the mathematics and the time budget.

Scoring

A correct answer earns 100–300 base points depending on the level, a speed bonus for answering inside the level's time budget, minus 20% of the base per hint, multiplied by a streak bonus of up to 1.5×. Wrong answers score 0 and the worked explanation is always shown.
